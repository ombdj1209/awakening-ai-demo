Live demo

A real conversation.

The agent on a live call: real-time voice, the avatar, and an emotional read of how things are said, not just what is said.

  • Hears tone, not just words. “Yeah, everything is fine,” said in a distant voice, gets: “Well, you don’t sound particularly fine.”
  • Answers fast. About 1.5 seconds from the person’s last word to the agent’s first, measured in this recording.
  • Has a presence. Voice and a customizable avatar that react to the conversation.
